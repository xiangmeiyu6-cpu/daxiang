# Operating strategy framework

## 1. Product brief

- Brand and product identity.
- Category and regulatory identity.
- One-sentence positioning.
- Primary and secondary audiences.
- Core need, scenario, and purchase barrier.
- One hero idea and three support ideas.

## 2. Product and SKU architecture

Build a ladder with roles, not arbitrary discounts:

| SKU | Role | Price | Unit price | Gift | Audience | Margin/constraint |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|

Recommended roles: trial, hero, stock-up, gift, subscription/repeat. Validate quantity arithmetic, gift equivalence, inventory, postage, and platform price rules.

## 3. Search and title strategy

- Core category term.
- Ingredient/material term.
- Specification/form term.
- Audience/scenario term when compliant.
- Brand term.

Provide one primary title and 3-5 test variants. Avoid keyword stuffing and unsupported superlatives.

Each image must have one job. Keep copy legible on mobile and match actual product identity.

## 5. Detail-page sequence

Default 12-screen architecture:

1. Product identity and positioning.
2. Audience recognition or daily scenario.
3. Formula/material overview.
4. Hero ingredient/material detail.
5. Supporting ingredient/material group A.
6. Supporting ingredient/material group B.
7. Product texture, structure, or real experience.
8. Usage method and scenario.
9. Specification and package contents.
10. SKU, price, gifts, and promotion.
11. Real trust and label evidence.
12. Purchase note, restrictions, and close.

Reorder based on the reference product's proven mechanism and the new product's evidence. Never fill trust sections with invented certificates or numbers.

## 6. Content and advertising matrix

Create at least these lanes:

- Offer-first product-card creative.
- Product/ingredient education.
- Texture, unboxing, or demonstration.
- Real user scenario.
- Objection handling.
- Comparison without disparagement.
- Search-intent content.

For each lane provide hook, first three seconds, proof shot, CTA, compliance note, and target metric.

## 7. Test plan

Define:

- Hypothesis.
- Single changed variable.
- Audience/placement.
- Minimum evidence threshold.
- Primary metric and guardrail metric.
- Scale, iterate, or stop rule.

Do not recommend scaling from CTR alone. Connect click quality to conversion, refund, repeat purchase, and contribution margin where data exists.
