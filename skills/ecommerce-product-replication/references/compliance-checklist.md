# Compliance and evidence checklist

Treat this as a research checklist, not legal advice. Verify current rules from government, regulator, platform, standards body, or the user's counsel.

## Evidence integrity

- Do not invent sales, market share, patents, certifications, endorsements, test results, origin, formula amount, or user outcomes.
- Do not convert packaging decoration into proof.
- Quote the exact scope and date of certificates or reports.
- Mark platform-visible facts separately from seller claims.
- Preserve links, screenshot names, and access date.

## Ordinary food and health-adjacent products

- Do not claim disease prevention, treatment, cure, or drug substitution.
- Do not claim health functions reserved for approved health foods.
- Avoid body-condition promises such as detox, fat loss, edema removal, immunity improvement, sleep treatment, gastrointestinal treatment, or rapid physiological change unless the category and evidence legally support them.
- Verify whether every ingredient, plant part, dosage/use, product category, production region, and sales scope is permitted.
- Use actual label language for ingredients, net content, storage, usage, allergens, producer, permit, and standard.
- Keep consumer insight such as “people worried about dampness” out of public efficacy claims when the product is ordinary food.

## Advertising and platform claims

- Check current platform rules for title, main image, livestream, paid ads, creators, and prohibited words separately.
- Avoid absolute or unverifiable terms: best, number one, national level, zero risk, guaranteed, immediate, permanent.
- Ensure price anchors, gifts, scarcity, countdowns, and comparison prices are real and configured in the platform backend.
- Do not fabricate expert, doctor, hospital, laboratory, government, or media endorsement.

## Visual and IP review

- Confirm rights to user-supplied logos, packaging, photography, people, and trademarks.
- Reference composition, hierarchy, or mood; do not reproduce another brand's distinctive packaging or trade dress.
- Clearly mark AI concept visuals and replace label details with approved artwork before launch.
- Check every generated image for wrong characters, wrong quantities, wrong prices, extra products, and impossible ingredients.
